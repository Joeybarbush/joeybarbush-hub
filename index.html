§ 01

who is running this

// identity.read()
J

I am Joey. I am 21. I build cognitive infrastructure for individuals. Personal AI that does not rent you to big tech. Sole founder of HIPJOY, working from a desk in North Aurora, Illinois.

HIPJOY is a sovereign cognitive operating system. In plain English: a personal AI workspace where eleven specialized agents handle the different jobs of thinking. One agent is the archive. One is the equation. One is the broadcast. They cooperate. They keep my work continuous, private, and mine.

I do not have a team. I do not have venture capital. I have a notebook, a lattice of agents, and the conviction that the tools I want most do not exist yet, so I have to build them. The first public release shipped April 26, 2026 as a Claude Code plugin.

Right now I am building three things in parallel: IRON BLOOM, a hardware bench so the agents can run on local chips; SOVRA / LUX WEAVER, a policy gateway that routes which model handles which prompt; and RYTHMGUARDIAN, a biometric authentication seed that knows you by the rhythm of how you type.
